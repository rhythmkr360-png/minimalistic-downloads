# Privacy Policy — Minimal icon pack

**Effective date: 11 October 2026**

Minimal icon pack (`com.minimal.icons`) is an offline Android icon gallery. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

## Information handled by the app

The app does not require an account, and it has no network permission, advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK. The app itself does not send personal information or your activity to the developer or to an app server.

If you save icons as favorites, the app stores those choices in Android SharedPreferences in the app's private storage on your device. You can remove favorites in the app, or erase them by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

## Android backup and device transfer

Android's system backup may include app preferences, including saved favorites, when backup is enabled on your device. Android may store that backup with your Google Account and may restore it when you set up another device. This is controlled by Android and your device backup settings; the developer does not receive or have access to those backups. Review Google's [Android backup information](https://support.google.com/android/answer/2819582) and [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details about Google's handling of backup data. Device-to-device transfer behavior can vary by Android version and device manufacturer.

## Purchases and other apps

Minimal icon pack is distributed as a paid app through Google Play. Google Play processes the purchase and related account or payment information under Google's own terms and privacy practices; the app does not receive your payment card details. If you follow the app's guidance to apply icons in a launcher, that action is handled by the launcher you choose.

## Sharing, retention, and deletion

The app does not send favorites to the developer or share them with advertisers or analytics providers. Favorites remain in the app's private device storage until you remove them, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. If Android backup is enabled, backup copies are managed through your Google Account and Android's backup controls.

## Children's privacy

The app is a general-purpose customization utility and is not designed for children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated before the relevant app change is distributed. The effective date above will be revised when this page changes.
